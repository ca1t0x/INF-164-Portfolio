Why How I Met Your Mother?

It is my favourite show, and it changed the way I see life. The parts that stayed with me were about letting go, trusting that small moments can lead somewhere, accepting change, and appreciating the people beside me.

I chose four episodes because each one connects to something I want to carry into my own life. This website is a place to explain what those moments mean to me.
